Things to do around Arrochar

Walking, loch scenery, short drives and easy days around Arrochar, Tarbet and Loch Lomond.

Walk The Cobbler

The Cobbler is the best-known local mountain and a popular reason to stay in Arrochar. Check the weather, allow plenty of daylight and treat it as a proper hill day.

Explore Loch Long

Arrochar sits at the head of Loch Long, giving you an easy mix of village wandering, loch views and short drives.

Spend a day around Loch Lomond

Tarbet is close by and makes a straightforward base for Loch Lomond sightseeing, cruises and lochside stops.

Keep plans flexible

The weather can change quickly. One advantage of staying locally is being able to switch between walking, scenic driving and slower cottage days without losing hours in the car.
