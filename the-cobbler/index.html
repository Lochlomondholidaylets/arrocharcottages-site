The Cobbler walk near Arrochar

A practical local guide for visitors planning a walk in the Arrochar Alps.

Prepare for a proper hill day

The Cobbler, or Ben Arthur, is one of Scotland's most distinctive mountains. Check a mountain forecast, take suitable footwear and waterproof layers, and allow enough time for the conditions and ability of your group.

Stay close to the start

A nearby cottage makes an early start easier and gives you somewhere warm to return to after the walk. It also lets you keep a second day free for Loch Lomond or a gentler outing around Loch Long.

Build in a backup plan

If the tops are in cloud or the wind is stronger than expected, switch to a lower-level day instead. Arrochar, Tarbet and Loch Lomond all give you options without a long drive.
